Online Tuition

Live Online Tuition, Taught by Our Team

Small-group sessions taught live, for families who cannot get to Barkingside. We are taking names for the first groups now.

The Offer

What Online Sessions Are

Live teaching in small groups, following the same programmes we run at the centre.

How a session runs

Live, with a teacher there

Nothing is pre-recorded. A teacher is with the group for the whole session, so a child who is stuck can say so at the point they are stuck.

1 hour 45 minutes

The same length as a session at Barkingside. Long enough to teach something properly and then practise it while the teacher is still there.

Small groups, ages 6 to 16

Groups are kept small on purpose. Children are assessed first and taught at the level they are actually working at, rather than the level their year group implies.

What you need at home

Nothing unusual, but it is worth checking before the first session rather than during it.

✓A laptop or tablet with a camera and a microphone that work. A phone screen is too small to work from for this long.
✓A steady broadband connection. It does not need to be fast, it needs to stay up.
✓Somewhere quiet to sit for the session, with the screen at a sensible height.
✓Paper and a pencil. Children still work things out by hand. The writing does not move onto a screen.
✓Headphones, if the house is busy. Not essential, but they help more than people expect.

Worth Knowing

Online or the Centre?

Most of what we do carries over. Some of it does not, and it is better to know which is which before you decide.

What stays the same

  • The same teachers and subject specialists, all DBS-checked.
  • The same 1 hour 45 minute session.
  • The same programmes, from KS1 through to GCSE.
  • An assessment first, then teaching pitched at the level your child is actually working at.
  • Small groups, so a quiet child still gets spoken to.

What is different

  • You provide the room and the equipment, not us.
  • Written work is held up to the camera or photographed, rather than marked over a shoulder as it is written.
  • No travel. For families outside Redbridge, or with a younger child at home, that is often the whole argument.
  • A child who is easily distracted at home may do better in a room with other children. You know your child better than we do, and it is a fair reason to choose the centre instead.

Groups are small, so the number of places in each one is limited. We work through the list in the order names come in.

4

You decide then

Being offered a place is not the same as taking one. You see the detail, then say yes or no, and no is a perfectly good answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do online sessions start? ▾
We have not set dates yet. We are taking names now, and everyone on the list hears first, before places are offered more widely. That is the whole reason the list exists.
Is this live teaching or recorded lessons? ▾
Live. A teacher is with the group for the whole session and children can ask questions as they work. Nothing is pre-recorded.
How long is an online session? ▾
1 hour 45 minutes, the same as a session at our Barkingside centre. Long enough to teach something properly and then practise it while the teacher is still there.
What will my child need at home? ▾
A laptop or tablet with a working camera and microphone, a steady broadband connection, somewhere quiet to sit, and paper and a pencil. Children still work things out by hand, so the writing does not move onto a screen. Headphones help in a busy house.
Do the first two free sessions apply online? ▾
The two free sessions are for our Barkingside centre. We will confirm how fees work for online places before anyone on the list is asked to commit to anything.
Which year groups will the first groups cover? ▾
The programmes are the same as the centre, ages 6 to 16. Which groups open first depends on where the demand turns out to be, which is why the form asks for your child's year group.
Does joining the list commit me to anything? ▾
No. There is no payment and no obligation, and you can ask to come off the list at any time. It is a list of families to contact first, nothing more.
Where will the sessions actually run? ▾
Live sessions will run on a separate platform with its own sign-in, not on this website. We will send joining instructions well before the first session, so nobody is working it out on the night.
