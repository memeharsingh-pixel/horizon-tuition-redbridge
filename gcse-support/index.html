Timeline

The GCSE Two-Year Run

Drag through the stages, or pick one, to see where the work sits across Years 10 and 11.

Year 10 where grades are built

Most of the specification is taught this year, and this is where gaps open quietly. Nobody is panicking yet, which is exactly what makes it the cheapest time to fix things. Starting here is worth far more than the same hours spent in Year 11.

Year 11 mocks the first honest signal

Mocks usually land in November or December, and they are the first realistic picture of where your child actually stands. We work through the paper question by question and target the topics that lost the most marks.

May and June of Year 11 the exams

Final revision, past papers and exam technique: how to read a question, where the marks are, and what to do when you get stuck. The content work should be finished before this point, not starting.

🎯

Our GCSE Goal

Every GCSE student gets a personal target grade and a clear plan to work towards it. We aim for a grade 4 as a floor, and push beyond it wherever we can.
