Timeline

The Two-Year GCSE Journey

Explore how support is structured across Year 10 and Year 11 to keep learning on track.

Year 10 building strong foundations

Year 10 covers the bulk of the curriculum. Addressing target areas early builds steady subject confidence and saves valuable time during final Year 11 revision.

Year 11 mocks turning revision into results

The final year of GCSE preparation focuses on teaching the remainder of the subject content and sharpening exam technique. We help students turn subject knowledge into top marks through structured revision and targeted mock exam practice.

May and June of Year 11 the exams

Final revision, past papers and exam technique: how to read a question, where the marks are, and what to do when you get stuck. The content work should be finished before this point, not starting.
