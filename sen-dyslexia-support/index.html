Our Inclusive Approach

We adapt our delivery to accommodate different learning styles, so students who need extra support are able to thrive alongside their peers.

Multi-Sensory Teaching

We combine visual diagrams, clear verbal explanations and interactive problem-solving to help complex concepts click.

Structured and Predictable Sessions

Clear session outlines and step-by-step breakdowns reduce cognitive overload and help keep focus sharp.

Encouraging Environment

Small, structured group settings create a supportive space where students feel comfortable asking questions.

How We Support Specific Needs

If your child has a diagnosed learning difference or simply struggles with traditional classroom teaching, here is how we adapt our core lessons:

Dyslexia and Literacy Challenges

Step-by-step phonics, vocabulary building and reading analysis. Clear, easy-to-read visual resources and structured writing frameworks to help organise thoughts.

Dyscalculia and Maths Anxiety

Visual models and concrete examples before moving to abstract mathematical formulas. Focus on foundational core skills to replace exam anxiety with steady confidence.

ADHD and Focus Support

Fast-paced, varied task delivery to maintain high engagement. Clear instructions that break down larger assignments into manageable goals.

Delayed Readers

Targeted comprehension techniques matched to your child's current reading ability. Gradual vocabulary expansion to build reading fluency over time.
