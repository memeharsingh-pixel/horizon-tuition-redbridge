Terms & Conditions

Please read these in full before registering your child. Version 1.0-draft.

1. About these terms

These terms apply between Horizon Tuition Redbridge (“we”, “us”) and the parent or guardian registering a child (“you”). By completing registration you confirm you have read and accepted them. We recommend keeping a copy.

2. Registration and starting

A place is confirmed once we have received a completed registration and have space in a suitable group. Registration does not by itself guarantee a place in a particular group or at a particular time. We will always tell you which group and time your child has been offered before they begin.

3. Your free sessions

New students receive their first two sessions free on any programme, with no obligation to continue. If you decide not to continue after the free sessions, you owe us nothing.

4. Fees

Our current fees are £20 for a single session of 1 hour 45 minutes, or £35 a week for two sessions, which works out at £17.50 a session. Fees are published at horizontuitionredbridge.co.uk/pricing.

Fees are payable weekly in advance. We will give you at least four weeks' notice in writing before any change to fees.

5. Cancelling, pausing or leaving

We ask for two weeks' notice in writing (email is fine) if you wish to stop or pause your child's sessions. Fees remain payable during the notice period. Pausing for school holidays does not require notice where we have told you the centre is closed.

6. Your right to cancel within 14 days

Because you register online, you have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of registering, without giving a reason, under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. To cancel, email us at info@horizontuitionredbridge.co.uk.

If you ask us to begin sessions during that 14-day period, and you then cancel, you agree to pay for any sessions your child has already attended. Your free sessions remain free.

7. Missed and cancelled sessions

If your child cannot attend, please tell us as early as you can.

Sessions missed without at least 24 hours' notice are charged in full and cannot be made up. Where you give us at least 24 hours' notice, we will offer one catch-up session per term where a suitable space exists. Catch-up sessions cannot be carried over between terms and have no cash value.

If we cancel a session, you will not be charged for it, and we will offer a replacement session or credit the fee.

8. Arrival and collection

Please arrive on time. Children arriving late join the session already in progress and we cannot extend the session to compensate.

Children must be collected promptly at the end of their session. If a child has not been collected 15 minutes after the session ends and we cannot reach you or your emergency contact, we will follow our safeguarding procedure, which may include contacting Redbridge Children's Services.

9. Behaviour

We expect students to treat staff, other students and the centre with respect. Where behaviour disrupts other children's learning we will speak to you first and work with you to resolve it. In serious or repeated cases we may ask you to withdraw your child, and will refund any fees paid for sessions not yet taken.

10. Safeguarding

We take safeguarding seriously. All staff with unsupervised access to children hold an enhanced DBS certificate, and we follow Keeping Children Safe in Education and Working Together to Safeguard Children guidance. Our full policy, and how to contact our Designated Safeguarding Lead, is at our safeguarding page.

We may share information with statutory agencies without your consent where we believe a child is at risk of harm. We would normally tell you if we did so, unless doing so would put the child at greater risk.

11. Photographs and images

We will never photograph or film your child without your separate written consent, and consent for one use (for example our website) is not consent for another (for example paid advertising). You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us, and we will stop using the images going forward.

12. Medical information and emergencies

You must tell us about any medical condition, allergy, medication or additional need relevant to your child's safety or learning, and keep us updated if it changes.

In an emergency we will call the emergency services and then you. If we cannot reach you we will contact your emergency contact. By registering, you authorise us to seek emergency medical treatment for your child if we cannot reach you in time.

13. Your personal data

We collect and use your and your child's personal data to deliver tuition, keep children safe, and contact you. We handle it in line with UK GDPR and our privacy policy. You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold by emailing us.

14. Our responsibility to you

We will deliver tuition with reasonable care and skill. We cannot guarantee any particular exam result, grade or school place, as these depend on many things outside our control.

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot legally be limited.

15. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Where a change materially affects you we will give you at least four weeks' notice in writing, and you may end your agreement within that period without giving the usual notice.
