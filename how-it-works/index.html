The Process

From First Enquiry to First Session

A clear, transparent process. No pressure, no surprises.

1

Book Your Free Assessment

Complete the form on our Contact page or call us directly. Takes under 5 minutes. No payment required.

💡 The assessment is completely free and carries no obligation whatsoever.
2

Initial Evaluation

Your child attends a structured, friendly assessment session. Our teaching team evaluates current attainment, identify learning gaps, and understand your child's goals.

📋 Parents receive a verbal summary at the end of the session and a written overview within 48 hours.
3

We Place Your Child in the Right Group

Using the assessment, we place your child in a group working at the right level and agree what to focus on first. We will talk you through it and answer any questions.

4

Weekly Tuition Begins

Sessions are 1 hour 45 minutes. Structured, engaging, curriculum-aligned lessons with homework set and reviewed each session.

🎁 Your first two sessions are completely free, no charge until session three.
📅 Mon-Fri 4pm-6:30pm · Saturday 10am-3:45pm · Sunday 12pm-1:45pm
5

Ongoing Progress Monitoring & Reporting

We keep you updated on how your child is getting on, and adjust what we focus on as they develop.

Professional English, Maths and Exam Preparation Tuition for Ages 6 to 16. Delivered by qualified teachers and experienced subject specialists, all DBS-checked, in Barkingside, Redbridge.
