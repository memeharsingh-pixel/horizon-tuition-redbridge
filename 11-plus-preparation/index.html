11+ Preparation

Grammar School Success Starts Here

11+ preparation for GL Assessment papers and school-set entrance exams.

What the 11+ Tests

GL Assessment

Tests: Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, Mathematics, English.

School-set papers

Some schools set their own entrance papers rather than using a board. We prepare for these with past papers from the school and questions in the same style.

Timeline for Success

Drag the slider, or pick a year, to see what preparation looks like at each stage.

Year 4 foundation year

Groundwork. We shore up maths and English, then introduce verbal and non-verbal reasoning gently, so none of it feels like exam prep yet. Get this right and Year 5 is far more productive.

Year 5 the most important year

This is where the work happens. Because the exam falls in September, Year 5 is the last full year of preparation your child gets. Full topic coverage, timed practice and mock papers, with enough runway left to fix what they expose.

Year 6 exam in September

The exam is sat in mid-September, in the first few weeks of Year 6. Everything at this stage happens over the summer before: final mock exams, exam technique and keeping calm.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should we start 11+ preparation? ▾
The exam is sat in mid-September of Year 6, so Year 4 and Year 5 are the preparation years and Year 5 is the most important of them. Year 4 gives a relaxed two-year run; the start of Year 5 is the latest point to begin a full programme. If your child is already in Year 6 the exam is only weeks away, so talk to us and we will be honest about what is realistic.
Which grammar schools can you prepare for? ▾
We prepare for all selective schools accessible from Redbridge, including Ilford County High, Woodford County High, Bancroft's School, and others.
Do you offer mock exams? ▾
Yes. Mock exams under timed conditions are a core part of our 11+ programme. We review results with parents and use them to target remaining gaps.
