Our Story

Built on a Simple Belief: Every Child Deserves Expert Teaching

Horizon Tuition Redbridge was founded on a simple observation: most children who fall behind are not struggling with the whole subject, they are missing a handful of specific things nobody has had time to go back and fix. Our job is to find those gaps and close them.

Every child is assessed individually before they start, taught in a small group by qualified teachers and experienced subject specialists, and given a written plan that is reviewed each term. We are based in Barkingside and work with families across Redbridge, Ilford, Seven Kings, Woodford and the surrounding area.
