What Actually Happens

Most tuition centres will not tell you this plainly, so here it is, start to finish.

Before you start
01

We assess first

Before anything is booked we sit your child down and work out where they actually are. Not what year they are in, what they can currently do.

02

They join a small group

Matched on age and on ability, so nobody is the lost one at the back and nobody is sat bored having already got it.

03

One hour forty five, every week

Long enough to teach something properly and then practise it. Homework is set and marked every week, not handed out and forgotten.

04

You hear how it is going

We tell you what your child is finding hard and what has clicked. The first two sessions are free, so you see all of this before you pay.
