Who We Are

Horizon Tuition Redbridge, 5 State Parade, Barkingside, IG6 2EY. Contact: info@horizontuitionredbridge.co.uk

Data We Collect

We collect contact information (name, email, phone) and children's educational information (year group, subject needs) submitted via our enquiry form, plus anonymised analytics data via Google Analytics 4.

How We Use Your Data

To respond to your enquiry, arrange and deliver tuition, send progress updates, and (with consent) send marketing communications. We never sell your data to third parties.

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact info@horizontuitionredbridge.co.uk to exercise these rights.
